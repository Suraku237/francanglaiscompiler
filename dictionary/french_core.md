# French – English

*Core vocabulary reference used for the French half of Camfranglais code-mixing.*

This list is deliberately small. It covers the French words that actually surface in
Camfranglais speech: pronouns, auxiliaries, the handful of verbs that get inserted into
otherwise English/Pidgin frames, and everyday nouns. The POS column is the grammatical
category the analyzer assigns to the word.
