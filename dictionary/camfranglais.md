# Camfranglais – English

*A core vocabulary reference*

Camfranglais is the urban youth speech of Cameroon, built by mixing French with English, Pidgin and words drawn from Cameroonian languages such as Duala, Bamileke and Ewondo. This list gathers widely used terms with their English meanings, grouped by theme, with a note on where each word appears to come from.

**Two cautions.** Camfranglais changes fast and varies by city, generation and social group, so some items here will sound dated to a young speaker in Douala while others are very much alive. And spelling is unsettled: the same word turns up as *tchop / chop*, *njoh / ndjoh*, *kwatt / quat*. The origins given are indicative rather than settled etymologies.
