# Cameroonian Pidgin – English

*A core vocabulary reference*

A working list of frequently used Cameroonian Pidgin (Kamtok) words and expressions with their English meanings, grouped by theme so that related vocabulary sits together.

**On spelling:** Pidgin has no single official orthography. The spellings here follow a common phonetic convention, but you will often see the same word written differently elsewhere (*pikin / picken*, *wahala / wahalla*, *mimbo / mimboh*). Meanings also shift by region and by context, and many words carry a wider sense than a one-line gloss can hold.
