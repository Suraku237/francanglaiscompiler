# Supplementary lexicon

Items the analyzer needs that the two main dictionaries do not list: determiners,
demonstratives, a few high-frequency street words, and the abbreviations and slang that
turn up in Yaoundé speech. Keeping them here leaves `dictionary/pidgin.md` and
`dictionary/camfranglais.md` untouched as reference documents.

A heading of the form `## LANGUAGE: topic` sets the language for the rows below it.
