# Supplementary Camfranglais lexicon

Items the analyzer needs that `dictionary/camfranglais.md` does not list: the urban
vocabulary of Yaoundé transport, utilities and university life, plus a few fillers and
slang terms. Keeping them here leaves the reference dictionary untouched.

A heading of the form `## LANGUAGE: topic` sets the language for the rows below it.
