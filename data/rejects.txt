# Sentences that MUST be rejected by the parser.
#
# Every grammar has to be shown to reject as well as accept - these are the
# negative test cases for the 'which sentences are accepted or rejected' part of
# the project. Each line states the reason it is ungrammatical.

# noun pile-up with no predicate and no determiner structure
mbourou mbourou mbourou taxi taxi

# preposition with nothing to attach to
avec avec avec le

# tense marker with no verb and a stray determiner
il a le le

# adjective stack in front of a bare noun (the grammar requires a determiner)
gros gros chaud moto est la

# determiner with no head noun
le chaud le gros le

# question word followed by a determiner and nothing else
combien le
